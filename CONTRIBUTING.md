# Contributing

veil is source-available under the [PolyForm Strict License 1.0.0](LICENSE) (see also [`ADDITIONAL-PERMISSIONS.md`](ADDITIONAL-PERMISSIONS.md)), and it does **not accept contributions**: no pull requests, no patches, no issues. This is a decision about the project, not about you or your idea. Pull requests are closed without review.

What is welcome:

- **Reports of how veil works on your machine** (which compositor, which graphics card, which distribution, what worked and what did not): [Discussions, category "Reports"](https://github.com/ordreth/veil/discussions/categories/reports). [`docs/troubleshooting.md`](docs/troubleshooting.md#reporting-a-problem) says what to include. Reports are read, but nothing is promised in return: no answer, no fix, no date.
- **Security problems**, privately, as [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) says. Not in a discussion.

Everyone who takes part in the discussions follows the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
